The ten-minute routine

You know the feeling. A word looks familiar on the page, then disappears when you need it in a conversation. Practice bringing it to mind and using it in a sentence of your own.

  1. 2 min

    Collect three useful words

    Pick words or phrases you can imagine using this week. Keep the sentence you found each one in. If you collect run into, save “I ran into Maya at the station” so you remember how the phrase works.

  2. 4 min

    Recall before you look

    Cover the phrase and read a short clue, such as “meet someone by chance.” Say the phrase aloud, then check it. If you hesitate or miss it, try again after a different card.

  3. 4 min

    Put each word to work

    Write or say one true sentence with each phrase. For run into, think of someone you’ve seen unexpectedly and explain where it happened.

When to review

As a starting schedule, revisit today’s words tomorrow, in three days, and after a week. Bring a difficult word back into tomorrow’s practice. Give a word you recall easily a few more days before the next check.

When you need fresh words for the next round, browse English vocabulary practice and pick three expressions you can use in your own sentences.
